When You Contact Us

Please include enough information for us to understand the issue and help without unnecessary delay.

  • Include your full name and the email used for your purchase when contacting order support.
  • Include the product, song, album, or page involved.
  • Describe what happened and what you expected to happen.
  • Do not send passwords, full card numbers, or sensitive banking information.
