AWAKENED BY YAH MUSIC

Short Bio

AWAKENED BY YAH MUSIC is a truth-based soul and R&B artist creating music rooted in endurance, obedience, and calling on the Name of YAH. Through emotionally rich sound and purpose-driven lyrics, the music speaks to the awakening of Yasharal and those walking a disciplined, set-apart path in a noisy world.

Long Bio

AWAKENED BY YAH MUSIC is a truth-based soul and R&B artist whose music is rooted in endurance, awakening, and alignment with the Word. Each song is crafted to strengthen the Remnant — those seeking truth beyond tradition and walking a disciplined, set-apart path. Blending emotional depth with scripture-anchored themes, AWAKENED BY YAH MUSIC delivers sound that ministers, challenges, and restores.

The mission is not fame, but faithfulness — using music as a tool of remembrance, encouragement, and truth in a generation surrounded by noise.

Press Release

AWAKENED BY YAH: Press Release

A Truth-Based Music Movement Uniting Revelation, Obedience, and AI-Assisted Creativity

Press Inquiry

Mayaka’al Ben Yasharal, founder and visionary behind AWAKENED BY YAH, is leading a set-apart musical movement rooted in revelation, obedience, and purpose. Founded in 2021, AWAKENED BY YAH was birthed from a personal awakening and a calling to create music that uplifts the Name of YAHUAH, restores identity, and calls the scattered of Yasharal back to the ancient path.

After coming into the Truth and walking in obedience to the commandments of YAHUAH and the testimony of YAHUSHA for over four years, Mayaka’al Ben Yasharal and his family began guarding the ancient way — a life marked by repentance, love, and restoration. What began as ministry through TikTok exhortations and YouTube teachings has since expanded into music — a universal language used to reach those whom YAHUAH is awakening in this generation.

Ruah-Led Music, AI-Assisted Production

AWAKENED BY YAH uniquely blends Spirit-led songwriting with ethical, purpose-driven AI-assisted music production. AI is used as a creative tool — not a replacement for obedience, revelation, or conviction — allowing the project to steward creativity, overcome industry barriers, and express soundscapes that serve the message rather than the system.

Through this approach, AWAKENED BY YAH has also developed multiple AI-assisted artist expressions, each created to carry specific tones, moods, and musical styles while remaining unified under one spiritual mission and doctrinal foundation.

“AI is not the source — the Ruah is. These tools are simply vessels. The message, the obedience, and the calling come from YAHUAH.”

Each artist expression exists to amplify the mission, not dilute it — ensuring that every release remains reverent, intentional, and anchored in Truth.

Music Rooted in Restoration and Identity

Every song released under AWAKENED BY YAH carries messages of:

  • Restoration
  • Identity in YAHUAH
  • Repentance and obedience
  • Return to the ancient way

The lyrics are inspired by real experiences, prayer, Scripture, and the leading of the Ruach — never trends, commercial pressure, or worldly validation. The result is Truth-Based Soul, Set-Apart R&B, and Spiritual Awakening Music designed not just to be heard, but to be felt.

This sound stands apart from the world’s system, offering music for those who sense the call to wake up, remember who they are, and walk differently.

Mission & Vision

  • Genre: Truth-Based Soul • Set-Apart R&B • Spiritual Awakening Music
  • Mission: To awaken the chosen of Yasharal and uplift the Name of YAHUAH through music that restores the ancient path
  • Founded: 2021
  • Distinctive Approach: Ruah-led songwriting combined with ethical AI-assisted production and multiple AI artist expressions
  • Rooted In: A family’s awakening, obedience to YAHUAH, and the testimony of YAHUSHA

Through music, media, and ministry, AWAKENED BY YAH exists to awaken, uplift, and unite — praying that every listener not only hears the sound, but recognizes the call of YAHUAH drawing them closer.
