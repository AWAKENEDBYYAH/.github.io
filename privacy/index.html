Section 01

Introduction

AWAKENED BY YAH MUSIC GROUP ("AWAKENED BY YAH MUSIC," "we," "us," or "our") operates awakenedbyyahmusic.com and the pages, tools, and services made available through that website (collectively, the "Site").

We respect the information entrusted to us and seek to handle it responsibly, transparently, and only for legitimate website, music, customer-service, ministry, security, and communication purposes.

By using the Site, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy & Data Practices notice. Where consent is legally required, we will seek it through an appropriate form, checkbox, or other clear action.

Section 02

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Depending on how you use the Site, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Your name, email address, and message when contacting us.
  • Your name, optional email address, rating, review title, review text, release selection, and consent when submitting a listener review.
  • Your name, email address, and consent choice when joining the Remnant List or another AWAKENED BY YAH MUSIC email list.
  • Order, delivery, billing, and transaction-related information submitted through Payhip, Stripe, or another approved payment or commerce provider.
  • Information you voluntarily provide through live chat, customer support, or direct email communication.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit or interact with the Site, certain technical information may be collected automatically by us or our service providers, including:

  • IP address or an approximate location derived from it.
  • Browser type, operating system, device type, screen size, language, and user-agent information.
  • Pages visited, referring page, navigation paths, session duration, clicks, scrolling, and general interaction data.
  • Date and time of access, error information, and basic performance or security logs.
  • Cookie identifiers, local-storage identifiers, session identifiers, and similar technologies.
Section 03

How We Use Information

We may use information for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Site.
  • To display approved reviews and official responses.
  • To process purchases, provide downloads, confirm transactions, and respond to order questions.
  • To process one-time or recurring ministry support.
  • To respond to support, press, general-information, and review-related inquiries.
  • To send requested music, ministry, release, video, promotional, or website updates.
  • To understand general visitor activity, improve page performance, and identify technical problems.
  • To protect visitors, our systems, and our legal rights; prevent fraud or abuse; and comply with applicable law.

We do not sell personal information or email addresses in exchange for money.

Section 04

Listener Reviews & Official Responses

Reviews submitted through the Site are held for administrative review before publication.

If approved, the following information may be displayed publicly:

  • Your submitted display name.
  • Your star rating.
  • Your review title and review text.
  • The release you reviewed.
  • The approximate submission or approval date.
  • Labels such as Verified Listener, Featured, New, or Review of the Month.
  • An official response from AWAKENED BY YAH MUSIC GROUP.

A reviewer's email address is used only for administrative, verification, support, or follow-up purposes and is not intentionally displayed publicly.

We may decline, remove, or restrict reviews that contain private information, unlawful material, threats, spam, impersonation, harassment, or content unrelated to the reviewed music or service. We may make limited formatting changes that do not materially alter the reviewer's meaning.

To request removal or correction of a review you submitted, contact us using the support address listed below. We may request reasonable information to verify the request.

Section 05

Email Subscriptions

When you join the Remnant List or another AWAKENED BY YAH MUSIC mailing list, we may use your email address and optional name to send:

  • New music and album announcements.
  • Official video and website updates.
  • Digital-store news, offers, or discounts.
  • Ministry updates and important announcements.
  • Administrative messages related to your subscription.

Subscription forms will identify the information requested and include an affirmative consent action. You may unsubscribe at any time by using the unsubscribe method included in an email or by contacting support@awakenedbyyahmusic.com.

Transactional messages—such as receipts, download notices, account-security messages, or support replies—may still be sent when necessary even after you unsubscribe from promotional messages.

Section 06

Purchases & Ministry Support

Digital products, physical products, and ministry-support payments may be processed by third-party commerce and payment providers such as Payhip and Stripe.

Those providers may collect information necessary to process a transaction, including your name, email address, billing details, payment method information, transaction amount, location, IP address, and fraud-prevention signals. Their collection and handling of information are governed by their own privacy notices and contractual obligations.

AWAKENED BY YAH MUSIC does not intentionally receive or store your full payment-card number through the Site. Payment credentials are handled by the applicable payment processor.

We may receive limited transaction information, such as your name, email address, products purchased, payment status, transaction identifier, support tier, and dates necessary to fulfill your purchase, maintain records, provide customer support, prevent fraud, or meet legal and tax obligations.

Section 07

Cookies, Local Storage & Similar Technologies

The Site and its service providers may use cookies, browser local storage, session storage, pixels, tags, and similar technologies to:

  • Remember preferences and session choices.
  • Maintain secure administrative authentication.
  • Prevent duplicate helpful-review votes.
  • Remember ambient-audio or interface preferences.
  • Measure visitor activity and website performance.
  • Operate live chat, payment, and embedded services.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, technical errors, or security risks.

Browser settings may allow you to block or delete some cookies and storage data. Doing so may affect login, checkout, preferences, live chat, reviews, or other Site functions.

Section 08

Analytics & Website Technology

We use technology providers to host, secure, analyze, and operate the Site. Current providers may include:

GitHub Pages Website hosting and delivery of public site files.
Google Firebase & Firestore Authentication, review storage, live-visitor tools, database functions, and administrative features.
Microsoft Clarity Website-usage insights, session interaction data, and performance analysis.

TikTok & Other Linked Platforms External music-video, social-media, and promotional destinations opened through links on the Site.

These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies. We encourage visitors to review the privacy terms of services they choose to use.

Section 09

How Information May Be Shared

We may share limited information:

  • With service providers that host, secure, process, analyze, support, or deliver Site functions.
  • When you direct us to share information or use a third-party service.
  • To complete purchases, process payments, deliver downloads, or provide customer support.
  • To comply with law, legal process, court orders, or lawful government requests.
  • To investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, threats, or violations of Site rules.
  • In connection with a lawful restructuring, transfer, or continuation of the website or music operation, subject to appropriate protections.

We do not authorize service providers to use information supplied through the Site for purposes unrelated to the services they provide to us, except as permitted by their own lawful role or required by law.

Section 10

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, maintain website and transaction records, provide support, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, enforce agreements, and comply with legal, accounting, or tax requirements.

Retention periods vary by category. For example:

  • Approved reviews may remain public until removed by us or following a verified request.
  • Rejected or deleted reviews may be removed sooner, though limited logs or backups may persist temporarily.
  • Email-subscription records may be kept until you unsubscribe and for a reasonable period afterward to honor suppression or compliance requirements.
  • Purchase and payment records may be retained longer where required for tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, or legal purposes.
  • Analytics, security, and technical logs may be retained according to operational needs and provider settings.
Section 11

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information, including authenticated administrative access, restricted database permissions, secure third-party payment processing, and website-service security controls.

No method of internet transmission, browser storage, cloud hosting, or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should not send passwords, full payment-card numbers, banking credentials, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information through reviews, general email, or live chat.

If you believe information associated with the Site has been accessed or used improperly, contact us promptly.

Section 12

Your Choices & Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and reasonable verification, you may ask us to:

  • Confirm whether we maintain personal information about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information you submitted.
  • Remove or anonymize a review you submitted.
  • Unsubscribe you from promotional email communications.
  • Delete certain information that we are not legally or operationally required to retain.
  • Explain how information associated with your request has been used.

We may need information sufficient to verify your identity, ownership of an email address, or authorship of a review. We may deny or limit requests where permitted or required by law, where verification is not possible, or where retention is necessary for security, fraud prevention, transactions, legal claims, tax, or compliance.

You may also manage cookies and local-storage information through your browser and may stop promotional email by using the available unsubscribe process.

Section 13

Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information online from children under 13 without appropriate parental authorization where required.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information through the Site, contact us so that we can review and address the matter.

Section 14

International Visitors

The Site is operated from the United States and uses service providers that may process information in the United States and other countries. Privacy and data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.

Where applicable law grants additional rights, you may contact us to make a request. Third-party service providers may offer separate privacy portals or request procedures for information they control.

Section 15

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the Site, our services, technology providers, legal requirements, or data practices.

The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Material changes may also be communicated through the Site or another appropriate channel.
